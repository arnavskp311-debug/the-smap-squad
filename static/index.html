🛰️ Verified NASA Earth Observation Constellations Direct Ingestion & Scientific Grounding
💧 NASA SMAP L4 (SPL4SMGP)
Measures 0–5 cm surface and 0–100 cm root-zone volumetric soil moisture globally every 3 hours at 9 km EASE-Grid. Serves as the 40% primary weight in our drought index.
🌧️ NASA GPM IMERG (GPM_3IMERGDL)
Global daily calibrated precipitation derived from active radar and passive microwave radiometry. Accounts for 30% of our multi-satellite water stress score.
☀️ MODIS MOD16A2 (Terra)
Global 8-day composite terrestrial evapotranspiration (ET) and latent heat flux at 500m resolution. Captures atmospheric water pull and crop transpiration losses (20% weight).
🌿 Landsat 8/9 OLI / TIRS
30-meter high-resolution multispectral imagery delivering NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index), SAVI, and Land Surface Temperature (LST) for canopy vigor audits.
🌡️ NASA POWER DAV REST API
Live agro-climatology REST endpoint providing daily 2-meter air temperature, relative humidity, and wind speed parameters for active district centroids.
🌐 NASA GRACE-FO
Measures regional monthly gravity variations to derive deep groundwater equivalent water thickness anomalies, confirming long-term aquifer depletion trends.
🚨 The Punjab Groundwater Crisis
• Fossil Aquifer Over-Extraction: Over 150 blocks in Punjab are officially classified as "Over-Exploited", with groundwater levels dropping by 0.5–1.0 meter annually.
• Pumping Energy Drain: Free agricultural electricity has created over 100,000 high-power submersible tube-wells drawing huge coal-fired power from the state grid.
• Soil Degradation: Flooding alkaline soils with poor-quality tubewell water exacerbates sodicity (pH > 8.5) and organic carbon depletion (OC < 0.40%).
💡 The SMAP Squad Solution
• Early Warning Before Wilting: Catches root-zone water deficits up to 7 days before crop leaf-wilting shows up on optical cameras.
• Actionable Extension Prescriptions: Translates satellite science into farmer-friendly Hindi/Punjabi WhatsApp advisories and PAU Gypsum/FYM doses.
• Quantified Aquifer Preservation: Direct Seeded Rice (DSR) and mulching protocols save 1.8 Million Litres per 10 acres, extending deep aquifer lifespan.
