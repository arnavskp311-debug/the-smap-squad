🛰️ Verified NASA Earth Observation Constellations Direct Ingestion & Scientific Grounding
💧 NASA SMAP L4 (SPL4SMGP)
Measures 0–5 cm surface and 0–100 cm root-zone volumetric soil moisture globally every 3 hours at 9 km EASE-Grid. Serves as the 40% primary weight in our drought index.
🌧️ NASA GPM IMERG (GPM_3IMERGDL)
Global daily calibrated precipitation derived from active radar and passive microwave radiometry. Accounts for 30% of our multi-satellite water stress score.
☀️ MODIS MOD16A2 (Terra)
Global 8-day composite terrestrial evapotranspiration (ET) and latent heat flux at 500m resolution. Captures atmospheric water pull and crop transpiration losses (20% weight).
🌿 Landsat 8/9 OLI / TIRS
30-meter high-resolution multispectral imagery delivering NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index), SAVI, and Land Surface Temperature (LST) for canopy vigor audits.
🌡️ NASA POWER DAV REST API
Live agro-climatology REST endpoint providing daily 2-meter air temperature, relative humidity, and wind speed parameters for active district centroids.
🌐 NASA GRACE-FO
Measures regional monthly gravity variations to derive deep groundwater equivalent water thickness anomalies, confirming long-term aquifer depletion trends.
🚨 The Punjab Groundwater Crisis
• Fossil Aquifer Over-Extraction: Over 150 blocks in Punjab are officially classified as "Over-Exploited", with groundwater levels dropping by 0.5–1.0 meter annually.
• Pumping Energy Drain: Free agricultural electricity has created over 100,000 high-power submersible tube-wells drawing huge coal-fired power from the state grid.
• Soil Degradation: Flooding alkaline soils with poor-quality tubewell water exacerbates sodicity (pH > 8.5) and organic carbon depletion (OC < 0.40%).
💡 The SMAP Squad Solution
• Early Warning Before Wilting: Catches root-zone water deficits up to 7 days before crop leaf-wilting shows up on optical cameras.
• Actionable Extension Prescriptions: Translates satellite science into farmer-friendly Hindi/Punjabi WhatsApp advisories and PAU Gypsum/FYM doses.
• Quantified Aquifer Preservation: Direct Seeded Rice (DSR) and mulching protocols save 1.8 Million Litres per 10 acres, extending deep aquifer lifespan.
Punjab Farm Water Watch
FROM SPACE TO KHET · NASA SPACE APPS 2026
Punjab Farm Water Watch is an operational agro-hydrological diagnostic and early-warning platform developed for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 (Delhi NCR). Powered by six open NASA Earth Observation satellite constellations, it simultaneously monitors rootzone hydrological scarcity, flags hidden physical and chemical soil degradation, forecasts 7-day soil moisture drydown, and delivers trilingual agronomic advisories to preserve Punjab's critical food bowl and deep fossil aquifers.

🌍 1. The Challenge & Punjab Groundwater Crisis Critical South Asian Hotspot
Punjab is ground zero for the most severe groundwater depletion crisis in South Asia:
  • 0.5 – 1.0 m/year Groundwater Decline: Over 20+ years of satellite gravimetry, NASA GRACE / GRACE-FO have recorded over 3,122 km³ of human-driven freshwater loss across global agricultural hotspots. In Punjab, over 150 out of 167 administrative blocks are officially categorized as "Over-Exploited".
  • Global Food Security Linchpin: Despite occupying just 1.53% of India's landmass, Punjab contributes 47% of India's central wheat procurement pool and drives ~26% of global Basmati rice exports. A structural collapse in Punjab's water-soil ecosystem presents a systemic shock to global food security.
  • The Hidden Soil Quality Barrier: Even where tube-well irrigation is maintained, crop yields suffer from secondary salinization (EC > 4.0 dS/m), tubewell-induced sodicity (pH > 8.5), organic carbon depletion (<0.40%), and subsurface puddling hardpans at 15–20 cm depth.

🧮 3. Water Stress Scoring Engine (0–100) Weighted Multi-Satellite Calibration
Water Stress Score = 0.40·(SMAP Deficit) + 0.30·(GPM Deficit) + 0.20·(MODIS ET) + 0.10·(Landsat LST)
• SMAP Soil Moisture Deficit (40%): Scaled between Field Capacity ($\theta_{FC} = 0.36\text{ m}^3/\text{m}^3$) and Permanent Wilting Point ($\theta_{WP} = 0.10\text{ m}^3/\text{m}^3$).
• GPM Rainfall Deficit (30%): Evaluates cumulative 30-day precipitation against a 50 mm agricultural buffer threshold.
• MODIS Atmospheric Evaporative Draw (20%): Scaled linearly from 2.0 mm/day (low draw) to 7.0 mm/day (extreme atmospheric draw).
• Landsat Thermal Canopy Stress (10%): Scaled from 25.0°C (optimal canopy temperature) to 42.0°C (severe heat stress).
0 – 30: Green Zone
Adequate moisture. Cross-check NDVI for hidden chemical/physical soil anomalies.
31 – 60: Yellow Zone
Moderate stress. Delay flood irrigation by 3 days and deploy paddy straw mulching.
61 – 100: Red Zone
Critical drought deficit. Immediate transition to Direct Seeded Rice (DSR) & furrow irrigation.
🧪 4. Dual-Diagnosis Soil Quality Innovation SMAP vs. Landsat Divergence Gate
⚡ Diagnostic Matrix Logic Gate:
IF SMAP Water Stress is 0–30 (GREEN Adequate Water) AND Landsat NDVI < 0.42 / SAVI < 0.35 THEN: TRIGGER SOIL QUALITY ANOMALY ALERT!
When radar sensors confirm ample subterranean moisture but optical reflectances indicate stunted vegetative growth, the limiting factor is not water deficit, but severe chemical or physical soil degradation:
  • Secondary Sodicity: Tubewells drawing deep brackish water elevate soil pH above 8.5, causing clay dispersion and poor soil aeration.
  • Secondary Salinization: High electrical conductivity (EC > 4.0 dS/m) creates osmotic stress, preventing roots from absorbing available water.
  • Subsurface Hardpan: Repetitive tractor puddling in flooded paddy fields creates an impermeable plough pan at 15–20 cm depth.
🔮 5. 7-Day ML Forecaster RandomForest Regressor
• Trained on 7-day multi-satellite historical lags (SMAP rootzone moisture, GPM rainfall, NASA POWER daily temperature).
• Outputs a 7-day step-ahead outlook with a 90% confidence uncertainty envelope derived from ensemble tree variance.
• Automatically falls back to an empirical physical exponential soil drydown curve if historical telemetry is sparse.
💧 6. Quantified Aquifer Impact PAU Field Prescriptions
• 1.8 Million Litres Saved / 10 Acres: Direct Seeded Rice (DSR) saves ~20% irrigation water compared to traditional puddle-transplanted paddy.
• 1,420 kWh Power Saved / 10 Acres: Directly cuts coal-fired state grid load and farmer tubewell pump degradation.
• District Scaling: A 10% adoption across Punjab's 1.4 million tubewells preserves billions of litres and stabilizes falling water tables.
